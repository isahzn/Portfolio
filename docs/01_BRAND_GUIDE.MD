# Floza Brand Guide

## 1. Brand Identity

## Brand Name

**Floza**

Floza represents a modern AI automation company that helps businesses remove repetitive work, improve efficiency, and build smarter systems.

---

# 2. Brand Positioning

Floza is positioned as:

> A technology partner that builds practical AI systems and automation workflows for businesses.

Floza is not positioned as:

- A generic AI tool
- A chatbot company only
- A web design freelancer
- A low-cost developer

Floza should feel like a professional software company.

---

# 3. Brand Personality

The brand personality should be:

## Intelligent

Floza understands technology deeply and creates advanced solutions.

## Professional

Communication should be clear, confident, and business-focused.

## Innovative

The company uses modern AI and automation technologies.

## Reliable

Clients should feel that Floza can handle important business processes.

## Practical

Solutions should focus on real business problems, not unnecessary complexity.

---

# 4. Tone Of Voice

## Should Sound Like:

- Professional
- Clear
- Confident
- Helpful
- Technical when needed

Examples:

Good:

> "We build AI-powered workflows that automate repetitive business processes."

Good:

> "Turn manual tasks into intelligent systems."

---

Avoid:

Too casual:

> "We make cool AI stuff."

Too complicated:

> "We leverage revolutionary neural architectures to disrupt enterprise workflows."

Too sales-heavy:

> "The #1 AI automation company guaranteed to transform your business."

---

# 5. Visual Direction

## Overall Style

The website should look like:

- A futuristic software company
- A premium AI startup
- A modern SaaS platform

Reference feeling:

- OpenAI
- Linear
- Vercel
- Modern SaaS dashboards

Not copying their designs, only their level of simplicity and professionalism.

---

# 6. Color Direction

Primary style:

## Dark Background

Purpose:

- Creates a premium technology feeling
- Makes glowing elements stand out
- Matches AI/software aesthetics


## Accent Colors

Suggested:

Primary:
- Electric blue

Secondary:
- Purple

Supporting:
- White/light gray

Use colors carefully.

Avoid:
- Too many gradients
- Too many bright colors
- Rainbow effects

---

# 7. Typography

The website should use modern clean fonts.

Recommended:

Headings:
- Bold
- Large
- Strong

Body:
- Easy to read
- Medium spacing

Style:

Example:

Heading:

> Build smarter workflows with AI

Body:

> Floza creates automation systems that help businesses save time and operate more efficiently.

---

# 8. UI Style

## Cards

Cards should have:

- Rounded corners
- Glass effect
- Subtle borders
- Smooth hover animations


## Buttons

Buttons should feel:

- Premium
- Simple
- Clear

Examples:

Primary:

> Start a Project

Secondary:

> View Demos


---

# 9. Animation Style

Animations should be:

- Smooth
- Subtle
- Professional

Use:

- Fade-ins
- Sliding sections
- Hover effects
- Small interactive movements


Avoid:

- Constant movement
- Flashy effects
- Distracting animations

---

# 10. Image Style

Use:

- Software screenshots
- Dashboard previews
- Workflow diagrams
- Abstract AI visuals

Avoid:

- Generic stock photos
- Random business people images
- Unrelated graphics

---

# 11. Brand Messaging

Main message:

> Floza builds AI-powered systems that automate business operations.

Supporting messages:

> Automate repetitive work.

> Build smarter customer experiences.

> Turn manual processes into intelligent workflows.

---

# 12. Client Perception Goal

After visiting the website, a potential client should think:

"I understand what they do."

"They have real technical ability."

"They can build something useful for my business."

"They are worth contacting."

---

# 13. Design Rules

Always:

✓ Keep layouts clean  
✓ Prioritize clarity  
✓ Show real capabilities  
✓ Make demos impressive  
✓ Focus on business value  


Never:

✗ Overcomplicate pages  
✗ Add features without purpose  
✗ Make the website look like a gaming interface  
✗ Use fake achievements or fake clients  
✗ Claim results that are not proven
