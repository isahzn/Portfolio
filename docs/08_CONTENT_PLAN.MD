# Floza Content Plan

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the content strategy and written content requirements for the Floza website.

The goal is to make every page:

- Clear
- Professional
- Easy for businesses to understand
- Focused on converting visitors into leads

The website should explain business value first, and technical details second.

---

# 2. Content Principles

All Floza content should follow these rules:

## Be Clear

Visitors should understand what Floza does within seconds.

Avoid:

- Complex technical language
- Unnecessary AI terminology
- Long explanations

---

## Focus On Business Problems

Talk about:

- Saving time
- Reducing repetitive work
- Improving customer experience
- Organizing information

Not just:

- Models
- APIs
- Frameworks
- Programming

---

## Be Honest

Never claim:

- Fake clients
- Fake results
- Fake experience
- Guaranteed outcomes

Only display:

- Real projects
- Real capabilities
- Demo examples

---
